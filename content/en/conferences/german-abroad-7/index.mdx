## **German in the World: Language Use, Language Change, Language Policy**

*Organising committee: Katharina Dück, Siegwalt Lindenfelser, Heiko F. Marten, Albrecht Plewnia*

**Call for Papers**

We cordially invite you to the conference “German Abroad 7” at the Leibniz Institute for the German Language (IDS) in Mannheim. The conference will take place from **6 to 8 October 2027**. Like the six previous conferences in the series, it brings together research on German and on varieties closely related to German around the world.

We welcome contributions from the entire spectrum of research on German outside the core German-speaking area, for instance on structural characteristics, language contact and language change, sociolinguistic processes and cultural contexts, in both synchronic and diachronic studies.

This time, language policy will be a particular focus. Key questions include: Under what political conditions do German-speaking groups live? Where is German supported as a first language, where does it play an important role in the education system or in the media, and where are language maintenance and opportunities for acquisition under threat? Which state or private actors are relevant in these processes and contexts? And how do standard and regional varieties relate to one another?

In doing so, we wish to look beyond traditional groups of speakers and their varieties and to build a bridge towards a more general discussion of the role of German within a society. Only through the interplay of speakers of traditional varieties of German, “new” minorities (i.e. more recent emigrants, return migrants, or transmigrants), German-as-a-foreign-language contexts, and language-policy actors from the DACHL region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein) and from local institutions do those complex constellations emerge which make up the overall presence of German in a society, in the sense of “German in Country X”.

As always, the thematic scope of “German Abroad” is as broad as possible. We would particularly like to encourage early-career researchers to submit proposals for papers.

If you are interested, please send an abstract of approx. 250–300 words to **ga7\@ids-mannheim.de** by **28 February 2027**.
